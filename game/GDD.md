# Disney Destiny 8-Bit Adventure - Game Design Document

## 1. Overview
An 8-bit character-driven video game set aboard the **Disney Destiny** cruise ship.

## 2. Playable Characters
* **Riley (11 years old):**
  * Explores the ship's upper decks and pool.
  * Visits the AquaMouse water slide.
  * Hangs out at **Edge** (Tween club).
  * Watches movies at the theaters and plays in the big Theater.
* **Amelia (8 years old):**
  * Explores similar ship locations (pool, theaters, AquaMouse).
  * Hangs out at the **Oceaneer's Club and Lab** (instead of Edge).
  * **Special Mini-Game:** *Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique Makeover* 
    * Customize dress (color & style).
    * Customize hair style.
    * Choose crown and scepter.

## 3. Game Structure (Levels/Zones)
1. **Title Screen:** Implemented! (Welcome to the Disney Destiny).
2. **Character Select:** Implemented! (Choose between Riley or Amelia).
3. **Upper Decks (Hub World):** Implemented! (Massive zig-zag platforming layout).
    * **Deck 11:** Main Pool, Senses Spa, Marceline Market, Funnel Vision.
    * **Deck 12:** Quiet Cove, Toy Story Splash, Hero Zone.
    * **Deck 13:** AquaMouse Entrance.
    * **AquaMouse Ride:** Floating tube climb leading to a Top Deck, followed by an automatic high-speed diagonal water slide dropping into a Splashdown Pool on Deck 13.
    * **Physics Features Added:** 
      * One-way floating stairs for natural ship traversal (no getting stuck).
      * Water resistance in pools (slower speed, lower jumps).
      * Automated slide physics (forces player down and left).
4. **Kid's Clubs:** Edge (if Riley) / Oceaneer's (if Amelia). *(Pending)*
5. **Theaters:** Movie theater and the grand Walt Disney Theatre. *(Pending)*
6. **Boutique (Amelia only):** Customization mini-game UI. *(Pending)*
