# Disney Destiny - Issue #12 Storyboard & Level Flow

## 1. Global Navigation & Hub
- **Elevators:** Midship Elevator banks will be added to Decks 11, 12, and 13.
- **Interaction:** Pressing Enter (or tapping) the elevator bank opens an 8-bit in-game floor selection menu, allowing the player to travel to different decks or specific interior mini-games.
- **Availability:** All bespoke levels are unlocked and accessible immediately. Players can choose to explore the upper decks or hop right into an elevator/door.

---

## 2. Amelia's Levels (Age 8)

### A. Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique
- **Gameplay/UI:** An 8-bit RPG "shop" interface. Amelia stands by a mirror, and the player selects outfit parts (dress color/style, hair style, crown, scepter) from a vintage menu box.
- **Persistence:** Changes update her global sprite and persist when she returns to the Hub World.

### B. Oceaneer Club (Deck 2)
- **Layout:** Central Rotunda acting as the main stage, branching into four themed experiential wings.
- **Wings:**
  1. **Marvel Super Hero Academy:** Suit Fabrication Consoles (customize armor) & Avengers Training Simulator (AR/VR test floor mini-game against drones).
  2. **Star Wars: Cargo Bay:** Creature Wrangling Stations (Loth-cats, Porgs) & Datapad Diagnostic Terminal (balancing power levels to prevent containment breaches).
  3. **Walt Disney Imagineering Lab:** Ride Studio Drafting Stations (build a coaster) & Ride Testing Simulator.
  4. **Fairytale Hall:**
     - *Rapunzel's Art Studio:* Freehand painting/color mixing.
     - *Belle's Library:* Interactive story-choice branching with magic portrait.
     - *Anna & Elsa's Sommerhus:* Gesture-controlled ice-magic conjuring.
- **Entry point:** Accessed via the new elevator system (or visually via the Sugar Rush Spiral Slide from Deck 3 if navigating adjacent areas).

---

## 3. Riley's Levels (Age 11)

### A. Hero Zone (Deck 12)
- **Entrance:** Direct door access on Deck 12.
- **Gameplay:** A timed, Incredibles-themed 8-bit platforming obstacle course. Riley must jump over hurdles, dodge swinging obstacles, and race to hit a buzzer at the end for a high score.

### B. Edge Tween Club
- **Entrance:** Accessed via the new Midship Elevators.
- **Gameplay:** A social arcade lounge. Riley can interact with an arcade cabinet to play a "game within a game" (like a simple retro space shooter) to earn a top spot on the Edge High Score board.
